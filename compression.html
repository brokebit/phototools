Compression is not something the glass does. It comes from where you stand: the ratio of camera-to-subject distance to camera-to-background distance. A longer lens only forces you far enough back for that ratio to climb toward 1, where the background is drawn as large as the subject.

Plain-text summary

Distances are measured from the camera's sensor plane with the lens focused on the subject. The camera sits 1.5 m above the ground and is aimed to frame the subject, without tilting the verticals.
